LEGAL • PRIVACY POLICY

Privacy Policy

How Havoc collects, uses, and protects information when you add or use Havoc in your Discord server.

01 — Overview

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains what information Havoc may collect, why it is collected, and how it is handled. By adding, accessing, or using Havoc, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this policy.

This policy applies to all users, server owners, administrators, moderators, and members of servers where Havoc is present.

02 — Collection

Information We Collect

Havoc only collects the information needed to provide its features. Depending on how a server is configured, this may include:

  • Discord user IDs, server IDs, channel IDs, and role IDs.
  • Server settings and bot configuration data.
  • Moderation records such as warnings, bans, kicks, and timeouts.
  • Command usage information.
  • Event data used by logging, AutoMod, anti-raid, and anti-nuke features.

Note: Havoc may process message content in real time to provide features such as AutoMod and logging, depending on configuration.

03 — Usage

How We Use Information

Information collected by Havoc is used only for:

  • Providing moderation, security, automation, and logging features.
  • Detecting and responding to raids, nukes, spam, and abuse.
  • Saving and applying server configuration.
  • Maintaining, troubleshooting, and improving the service.
04 — Sharing

Data Sharing

Havoc does not sell personal information. Information is not shared with third parties except where needed to operate the service, such as hosting and infrastructure providers, or where required by law.

Havoc runs on Discord. Information handled by Discord is governed by Discord's own Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

05 — Retention

Data Retention & Deletion

Data is kept only as long as it is needed to provide Havoc's features. Server data may be removed when Havoc is removed from a server, or after a reasonable period of inactivity.

You can request deletion of data related to you or your server by contacting the Havoc team. Some records may be kept where needed for security, abuse prevention, or legal reasons.

06 — Security

Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect stored information from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. No online service can guarantee complete security, and we cannot promise that information will never be compromised.

07 — Servers

Server Owner Responsibility

Server owners and administrators decide how Havoc is configured in their server, including which events are logged and which moderation actions are automated.

Server staff are responsible for informing their members about any logging or moderation in use, and for complying with applicable laws and Discord's rules.

08 — Children

Children's Privacy

Havoc is intended for use on Discord and is not directed at children under the minimum age required by Discord. We do not knowingly collect information from users who are below that age. If you believe this has happened, please contact us.

09 — Choices

Your Choices

You are in control of how Havoc interacts with your information:

  • Server owners may remove Havoc from their server at any time.
  • Server owners may change or disable logging and moderation features.
  • Users may contact us to ask what data is held about them or to request its removal.
10 — Changes

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Updates take effect when published on this page unless otherwise stated.

Continued use of Havoc after changes are published constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

11 — Contact

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or want to make a data request, contact the Havoc development or support team through the official support channels.
